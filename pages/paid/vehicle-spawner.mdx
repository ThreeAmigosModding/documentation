# [Advanced Vehicle Spawner](https://threeamigos.shop/resources/advanced-vehicle-spawner)

Introducing TAM_VehicleSpawner. With our advanced vehicle spawner system, you can setup job specific vehicles with ease. Have total control over the vehicles your players use, with support for unlimited departments & vehicles.

- Extremely configurable, with dozens of config options you can make the resource how you want it to be.
- Create unlimited departments & unlimited vehicles with ease
- Support for vehicle count tracking, allowing you to limit the amount of vehicles that can be spawned at once.
- Support for purchasing vehicles, allowing you to charge your players when they spawn vehicles.
- Support for vehicle blips, giving the ability to have per vehicle blips enabled so your players know which vehicles they have spawned.
- Full localization with support for any language, including the UI.
- Support for target interaction to open the menu.
- Automatically detect & cleanup vehicles deleted externally.
- Support for spawning vehicles on the server, allowing full entity lockdown.
- Integrations with ND, QB, QBox, ESX, Standalone out of the box with support for any framework.
- Integrations with TAM_Fuel, renewed-fuel, ox_fuel out of the box with support for any fuel system.
- Integrations with qbx/qb vehicle keys, renewed-keys & wasabi carlock out of the box with support for any vehicle key system.
- Integrations with any notification system.
- Sleek custom UI made with Shadcn, with various config options.
- High quality code & optimization.

## Dependencies
- [ox_lib](https://github.com/communityox/ox_lib)
- [ox_target](https://github.com/communityox/ox_target) (or your own)
- Fuel Script [Optional]
- Vehicle Key Script [Optional]
- Artifacts version `16000` or higher. 
- Gamebuild version `2802` or higher.
- OneSync (Formerly OneSync Infinity) and **not** legacy.
