# Frequently Asked Questions  
Welcome to the Three Amigos Modding FAQ! Below you'll find answers to the most common questions we receive from our community.


## Do you offer refunds?
**No.** we have a strict **no-refund policy**.  
Because all of our products are digital and immediately accessible after purchase, we cannot issue refunds once the content has been downloaded.

If you ever run into issues with a resource, our support team will be happy to help troubleshoot!


## Why don't you support *[X]* resource?
Due to the massive variety of FiveM scripts and frameworks, it's impossible for us to offer native support for every single resource.

However, we **do provide extensive bridges, included integrations and integration hooks** to help you connect our products with nearly any script you prefer.  
If your setup requires something unique, feel free to reach out—our team is always improving compatibility options.


## Do you offer source access?
**No.** All of our paid resources include extensive bridges to integrate anything you might need, and in an attempt to combat leaking we do not offer source-access or "open source" version of our paid resources at this time. It's possible that this may change in the future, but as of the time of writing we do not.

If you need source access for a specific integration or modification, please check the product page or reach out to our support team for clarification.


## Why do you use escrow?
All of our paid resources utilize the FiveM asset escrow system to prevent unauthorized distribution and license verification.


## Do your scripts work with both ESX/QBOX/QBCore?
Mostly, you can view the compatible resources on the product page. All of our paid resources include a bridge for you to integrate any framework of your choosing.


## Do you provide installation support?
Yes!  
We offer detailed documentation with every resource, and our support team can help you with installation questions, troubleshooting, and integration advice.


## Can I use your scripts on multiple servers?
By default, you can only have 3 servers registered in the CFX portal. In order to run more than 3 copies of a resource, you will need to either reach out to CFX to obtain more server keys or create another account and purchase another copy of the resource.


## How often do you update your resources?
We regularly roll out updates to improve performance, maintain compatibility, and add new features.  
All updates and changelogs are announced in our [Discord](https://threeamigos.shop/discord).


## Where can I get support?
You can reach our support team through our [Discord](https://threeamigos.shop/discord).  
We're always happy to help you get the most out of your purchase.
